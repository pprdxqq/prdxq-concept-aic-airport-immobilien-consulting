# AIC Airport Immobilien Consulting — independent PRDXQ redesign concept

This is a public portfolio concept based on information available on AIC's German website and the City of Kelsterbach business directory. It was not commissioned, reviewed, or approved by AIC. The pages identify that status prominently and do not use AIC's original logo, photos, listing inventory, testimonials, or third-party marketing assets.

## Scope and verified details

- The company site describes real-estate consulting and project support, investments, and financing concepts.
- The City of Kelsterbach directory and AIC's current German contact/legal pages show Frankfurter Straße 24, 65451 Kelsterbach and phone +49 (0) 6107 98029.
- AIC's German contact page lists `mail@aic24.com` and appointments by arrangement. The municipal directory lists a different email (`info@aic24.de`); the concept uses the mailbox shown on AIC's own contact page.
- AIC's English property pages still show Frankfurter Straße 26 while its German pages and the municipal listing show 24. This discrepancy is documented in the concept and workbook.
- Service descriptions are summarized from public AIC pages. No specific properties, availability, fees, returns, qualifications, guarantees, or client outcomes were invented.

## Pages

- `index.html` — business overview and three verified business areas.
- `immobilien.html` — commercial property, private property, and project-management content.
- `investment.html` — investment and financing topics, without product, credit, or return claims.
- `region.html` — the Frankfurt Airport / Rhine-Main positioning and address-content check.
- `kontakt.html` — public contact information and direct phone, email, and map links.

## Technology and local preview

Plain semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript; no build step, external libraries, remote fonts, third-party images, analytics, embedded maps, or form backend. Open `index.html` locally or serve this folder using any static-file server. GitHub Actions deploys the root directory to GitHub Pages.

## Before any business use

The concept is not an official AIC site. Obtain the company's permission and validate the business name, branding, all content, current contact details, and applicable legal notices before presenting it as a client website. Confirm the address discrepancy with AIC. This static concept omits an official Impressum/Datenschutzerklärung and must not be repurposed as AIC's production site without the responsible operator's legal review.
